2. FINDINGS REQUIRING ACTION

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F1  [CRITICAL] THE ROOF-SAG AMPLITUDE WAS NEVER ESTIMATED
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The distinctive scientific hook across three plan versions — "search
for roofs, not holes" — assumes an intact tube roof produces a surface
depression large enough to measure. Six documents, ~23,000 words, and
the magnitude is never computed once.

I computed it. Clamped slab under self-weight, unit width:

    delta = rho * g * L^4 / (32 * E * h^2)

with rho = 3000 kg/m^3, g = 1.62 m/s^2:

    span L   roof h   E(GPa)    deflection
    ------   ------   ------    ----------
      65 m     26 m      50       0.08 mm     Marius Hills, intact
     100 m     26 m      50       0.45 mm
     300 m     26 m      50       3.6 cm      max span, intact rock
     300 m     26 m      10       18.2 cm     fractured
     300 m     10 m      10       1.23 m      thin roof, weak rock
     300 m     10 m       5       2.46 m      heavily damaged
     500 m     10 m      10       9.49 m      near-collapse

Against a NAC DTM vertical precision of roughly 0.2-1.0 m, and a
best-case corrected noise floor of 0.21 m RMSE (and that figure came
from 5 cm/px drone data over a built-up town, not 2-5 m lunar stereo):

  ELASTIC FLEXURE OF A COMPETENT BASALT ROOF IS UNDETECTABLE. By one
  to three orders of magnitude. At Marius Hills, sub-millimetre.

Worse, these are UPPER BOUNDS. Real lava tube roofs are arched, and
arches carry load largely in compression, reducing deflection further.
A flat-slab model flatters the project.

This does not kill the idea, but it forces a reframing that changes
what the project is looking for:

  (a) The mechanism must be restated. The detectable signal is NOT
      elastic flexure. It is CUMULATIVE DEGRADATION OF AN UNSUPPORTED
      SPAN over ~3.5 Gyr: roof spalling and rubble infill, regolith
      drainage into fractures, and progressive impact-induced damage
      preferentially weakening unsupported ground. Those produce
      metre-scale subsidence — and they are the recognised mechanism
      behind pit chains and sagging rille segments.

  (b) The detectable population is NARROW and identifiable in advance.
      Deflection scales as L^4/h^2. Detectability therefore requires
      thin roofs over wide spans in damaged rock — i.e. the
      NEAR-COLLAPSE regime. This is a genuine, quantitative,
      publishable targeting criterion that sharpens the search rather
      than weakening it. Marius Hills, with ~26 m roof and friable
      vesicular protolith, sits closer to this regime than
      Tranquillitatis with its competent dense basalt.

  (c) Gate G1 as written CANNOT BE EVALUATED. It asks whether sag
      exceeds the noise floor, but the plan supplies no sag amplitude
      to compare against. The gate has one measured term and one
      undefined term.

ACTION: insert WP0.5 — ROOF DEFORMATION FORWARD MODEL. Analytical plus
finite-element deflection across the plausible parameter space (span,
thickness, modulus, arch geometry, degradation state), producing a
predicted-amplitude distribution. Cheap: weeks, no new data, no
compute. It must precede WP1, because it defines what WP1 is testing
for. Reframe the hook as "detecting near-collapse unsupported spans,"
not "detecting roof flexure."

F2  [CRITICAL] THE NO-CHANGE REFERENCE SURFACE IS SELECTION-BIASED
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The kriged-correction transfer (I2/I3) depends on selecting reference
points where no true elevation change is expected. On Earth this was
roofs, runways and cleared roads — hard, textured, unambiguously
identifiable surfaces.

The plan proposes "flat mare panels with no mapped features." But
featureless low-texture terrain is precisely where stereo correlation
degrades and DTM quality is WORST. The reference points would therefore
be drawn from the highest-noise regions of the DTM, and the kriged
surface would encode correlation failure as if it were systematic
geometric error — then subtract it from everywhere, including the good
areas.

This inverts the terrestrial logic and could actively damage the
product. I introduced this without noticing the inversion.

ACTION: build the reference set from LOLA ground tracks and track
crossovers instead. LOLA is an independent instrument with independent
error characteristics, dense coverage, and no texture dependence.
Crossover residuals are the established lunar analogue of P1's cleared
roads. Validate that kriging against LOLA does not smooth real relief
by reporting the spatial-frequency comparison, exactly as the source
paper did.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F3  [MAJOR] VCI MAY NOT BE COMPUTABLE FROM LUNAR ORBITAL STEREO
-------------------------------------------------------------------
VCI measures the evenness of the vertical distribution of points WITHIN
a voxel column. It requires multiple returns per column.

A NAC stereo DTM is 2.5D — one elevation per cell. Converted to a point
cloud, every column contains exactly one point, so VCI is zero or
undefined everywhere by construction. The v4/v5 claim that VCI "spikes
at pit walls" is only true for a genuinely volumetric cloud.

It is salvageable, but conditionally: fusing the pre-gridded ASP
triangulated clouds (PC.tif) from MANY stereo pairs at differing
viewing geometries does produce multiple returns per column near steep
walls and overhangs, because different geometries see different parts
of the wall. That is a real and interesting method — but it is a
multi-pair fusion problem, not a drop-in inheritance, and it only works
where multi-geometry coverage exists.

ACTION: demote VCI from "inherited component" to "conditional method
requiring multi-pair cloud fusion." Add an explicit feasibility test in
WP0 on the Tranquillitatis multi-pair stack: compute points-per-column
statistics before claiming VCI applies. If median column occupancy is
1, the method is dead and should be cut rather than carried.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F4  [MAJOR] THE 30-SECOND RUNTIME CLAIM DOES NOT SCALE
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The plan cites ~30 s per dataset on a 16 GB laptop as independent
confirmation of the Tier-0 infrastructure claim. That runtime was for a
28.2 ha site. A single NAC DTM footprint is on the order of 100+ km^2 —
roughly four to five orders of magnitude more area. The inference does
not hold.

ACTION: remove the claim as stated. Tier 0 remains defensible on other
grounds (raster operations, pre-made DTMs), but benchmark actual
runtime on one full NAC DTM in WP0 and state the measured figure.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F5  [MAJOR] THE BUDGET IS A COMPUTE BUDGET PRESENTED AS A PROJECT
    BUDGET
-------------------------------------------------------------------
"Under $800 across 30 months" covers cloud instances only. It excludes:
the WP1 drone field campaign (travel, equipment, personnel, permits);
any salary or stipend; publication charges (Remote Sensing of
Environment and ISPRS open-access fees run to several thousand dollars
each, and the plan proposes six papers); conference travel; and the
terrestrial analog site access.

Presenting $800 as the project cost would read to a funding reviewer as
either naive or evasive.

ACTION: relabel explicitly as COMPUTE BUDGET. Add a separate indicative
cost envelope with the categories above, even if approximate. State
team size and FTE, which the plan never does anywhere.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F6  [MAJOR] LLTB-1 CANNOT BE OPENLY RELEASED AS CURRENTLY CONSTITUTED
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The flagship deliverable is an open benchmark. Its primary ground-truth
component is the NASA Planetary Pits and Caves Analog Dataset, which
the plan itself correctly flags as research/academic use only, with
commercial use or incorporation into a redistributed database requiring
explicit written author consent.

The plan flags the licence in the data section and then proposes open
release in the deliverables section without connecting the two. As
written, the flagship deliverable is blocked.

ACTION: one of three, decided in M0-M3, not later.
  (i)  Seek written consent from the dataset authors. Low cost, should
       be attempted immediately regardless.
  (ii) Build LLTB-1 only from openly-licensed sources (Corona/
       Lanzarote, Grotta di Monte Intraleo) plus own drone data, and
       cite the NASA set as a validation-only reference not
       redistributed.
  (iii) Release LLTB-1 as a processing pipeline plus manifest that
       regenerates the benchmark from sources users obtain themselves.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F7  [MODERATE] THE MATCHING RADIUS DEFEATS ITS OWN PURPOSE
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The plan sets a >= 30 m matching radius to respect Lunar Pit Atlas
coordinate accuracy. But candidate sag features are 60-300 m wide. A
30 m centroid tolerance against features of that size makes matching
near-automatic, and precision correspondingly near-meaningless.

ACTION: use polygon IoU matching for extended features, retaining
centroid-distance matching only for point-like pits. Report both.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F8  [MODERATE] THE CIRCULARITY CRITICISM APPLIES TO US TOO
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The plan states that scoring the ESSA baseline against the Pit Atlas is
circular because ESSA was trained on Atlas labels. Two problems. First,
ESSA's newly-surfaced skylights are genuine out-of-sample evidence and
the blanket statement understates it. Second, and more importantly, the
plan's own WP2 calibrates on Tranquillitatis, an Atlas pit — the same
circularity, uncriticised.

ACTION: state the shared limitation symmetrically. Define one Atlas
subset reserved for calibration and a disjoint subset never touched
until final evaluation, applied identically to our detector and to the
baseline.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F9  [MODERATE] SIX PAPERS IN 30 MONTHS IS NOT STAFFED
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The plan never states team size. WP3 explicitly requires geophysics
collaboration that is not identified. WP7 depends on a robotics partner
who is not named. Six first-author papers plus a benchmark, a platform,
a provenance graph and a target list, in 30 months, implies a team of
three to four.

ACTION: state FTE. If solo or a single PhD, cut to three papers
(benchmark, catalogue, fusion) and move WP4-WP6 to a stated follow-on.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F10 [MODERATE] THE DLR POSITIONING IS UNVERIFIED
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 12's positioning statement leans entirely on transferring an
in-house DLR pipeline and on an internal path to the DLR Institute of
Planetary Research. Nothing in the record establishes that the author
is at DLR. If not, the positioning is not merely weaker — it is
inaccurate, and "transfers that validated pipeline" becomes an
overstatement of relationship.

ACTION: verify. If unaffiliated, reframe as "builds on open-access,
openly-licensed methods published by DLR Jena" — still true, still
strong, and correctly attributed.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F11 [MINOR] NO COMPETITIVE-DISPLACEMENT CONTINGENCY
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The field is active; ESSA appeared in 2025. A competing roof-sag or
near-collapse detection paper published during WP1 would gut Paper 2
and the plan has no response.

ACTION: add a standing quarterly literature scan with a defined
pivot — toward the benchmark, the fusion methodology, or Mars, all of
which retain value independently.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
F12 [MINOR] "NOT COMPUTE-LIMITED" IS ASSERTED BEFORE IT IS TESTED
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 1's organising claim is well-argued but rests on the pre-made
DTM route. If WP2 finds that published DTMs do not cover enough
tube-relevant mare terrain, the project becomes compute-limited after
all, and the conclusion silently flips.

ACTION: make the M0-M3 index intersection an explicit gate condition,
with a stated minimum coverage threshold below which the infrastructure
strategy is revisited.

=====================================================================
4. ASSESSMENT
=====================================================================

The plan's reasoning quality is high and its intellectual honesty is
unusually good — the evidence hierarchy, the base-rate arithmetic and
the pre-registered null all reflect genuine discipline. Nothing in this
review overturns the project's core judgement that lunar void inference
is feasible, cheap, and under-explored.

But the plan verified everything except its own central premise. It
scrutinised GRAIL's resolution, Diviner's blindness, ESSA's
circularity, label scarcity and the base rate — and never once asked
how big the signal it was hunting actually is. That is a
characteristic failure mode: the hook was attractive, and attractive
hooks get elaborated rather than tested.

The good news is that the test is trivial, and the result improves the
project. Deflection scaling as L^4/h^2 converts a vague aspiration into
a quantitative targeting criterion: look for thin roofs over wide spans
in damaged rock, because nothing else moves enough to see. That is a
sharper, more defensible, more publishable project than v5 describes.

Revise and proceed.
