--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROJECT THESIS AND FRAMING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Working title:
  "Calibrated Multi-Evidence Inference of Lunar Subsurface Voids from Fused
   Surface Morphometry and Geophysics, Anchored in Terrestrial Analogs"

The founding idea: adapt a proven drone-forestry pipeline (SfM-MVS point
clouds -> DTM/DSM -> canopy height model -> feature stacking -> supervised
dead-tree classification) to satellite-derived 3D reconstruction of the Moon
for lava tube detection.

The single most important design decision, established in the feasibility
analysis, is this: a tree is ABOVE the surface; a lava tube is BELOW it.
Photogrammetry reconstructs only the illuminated outer envelope, so a lunar
point cloud contains zero direct information about a void 20-100 m down.
Therefore this project does NOT build a "detector." It builds a probabilistic
INFERENCE system whose output is P(void | all evidence) with calibrated
uncertainty, where the point cloud is one input among several.

The cleanest methodological bridge from forestry to the Moon: replace
CHM = DSM - DTM with a DEPRESSION-DEPTH RASTER computed as hydrological
sink-fill(DTM) minus DTM. This is the exact mathematical mirror of a canopy
height model and is the anchor of the whole method.

Positioning against prior art (all verified as published):
  - CNN pit detection exists (ESSA, Mask R-CNN, Icarus 2025).
  - Photogrammetric pit models exist (Zhou et al. 2024).
  - Radar conduit evidence exists at Mare Tranquillitatis (Carrer 2024).
  - GRAIL gradiometry and SELENE LRS candidates exist (Chappaz 2017,
    Kaku 2017, Zhu 2024).
Our novelty lives in five gaps: (1) multi-modal multi-scale fusion, (2) intact
roof-sag detection, (3) a terrestrial-to-lunar degradation ladder with a
detectability curve, (4) positive-unlabeled learning with conformal
calibration, (5) physics-informed stability screening.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. RESEARCH QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

RQ1  At what ground sample distance, sun geometry, and roof thickness does a
     lava tube's surface expression become undetectable? (Detectability curve.)
RQ2  Can intact-roof sag — a shallow, linear, laterally continuous residual
     depression — be discriminated from sinuous rilles, wrinkle ridges,
     graben, lava channels, and secondary crater chains at 2-5 m DTM posting?
RQ3  Does fusing NAC-DTM morphometry with Diviner thermal, Mini-RF radar,
     GRAIL gravity gradients, and SELENE LRS echoes yield better-calibrated
     void probabilities than any single stream, given a 3-4 order-of-magnitude
     scale mismatch (2-5 m vs 10-30 km)?
RQ4  Which candidate sites maximize expected information gain for
     LunarLeaper- or Moon-Diver-class missions?

3. DATA ARCHITECTURE (from the dataset assessment)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Primary substrate:  LROC NAC DTM collection (2-5 m posting, quarterly
  releases) filtered through SHAPEFILE_NAC_DTMS error metrics (relative
  linear error at 90% confidence, triangulation RMS). Usability 9.4/10.
Imagery:            LROC NAC EDR/CDR (>2.5M images, 0.5-2 m/px) fetched by
  product ID only — never mirrored (archive is ~579 TB EDR, >1 PB CDR).
  NAC Stereo Catalog identifies buildable pairs. 9.2/10.
Labels:             Lunar Pit Atlas / SHAPEFILE_LUNAR_PIT_LOCATIONS. Critical
  constraint: of ~281 pits, most are impact-melt; only ~15-16 mare and ~5
  highland pits are plausibly tube-related. Roughly 20 usable positives,
  zero verified negatives. 9.1/10.
Baseline:           ESSA model weights, training product list, and detection
  shapefiles (Zenodo/GitHub) — a baseline to beat, never a label source,
  because it was trained on the Pit Atlas (circularity risk). 8.9/10.
Trend surface:      SLDEM2015 (+/-60 deg, ~59 m/px, 3-4 m vertical). 8.7/10.
Mid-scale:          Kaguya TC observations and USGS ASP DTMs as Cloud
  Optimized GeoTIFFs on AWS — windowed HTTP reads collapse storage needs
  to near zero. 8.6/10.
Ground truth:       NASA Planetary Pits and Caves Analog Dataset (FARO TLS:
  King's Bowl, Indian Tunnel surface + cave interior, Fieg, Sheepridge;
  ~4.5 GB, .f32 format). RESEARCH/ACADEMIC USE ONLY — no commercial use or
  redistribution without written consent. Supplement with Corona/Lanzarote
  TLS and Grotta di Monte Intraleo open LiDAR. 8.5/10.
Hard negatives:     Hurwitz et al. 195-rille shapefile (drawn on Kaguya TC —
  expect small offsets vs LROC) and LU5M812TGT (~5M craters to 0.4 km) as
  the confuser inventory. 8.3 and 6.0/10.
Transfer:           MGC3 Mars catalogue (>1,000 candidates, CTX/HiRISE) for
  cross-body pretraining to escape the label famine. 8.2/10.
Confirmation-only layers: Diviner thermal (pits run ~100 K warmer at night,
  but thermal IR is blind to the cave behind the pit — confirmer, not
  detector), Mini-RF/DFSAR radar, GRAIL GRGM1200A/GL1500E gradients
  (10-30 km effective resolution only), SELENE LRS sparse tracks.
High-upside/high-friction: Chandrayaan-2 OHRC (0.25 m GSD) and TMC-2 stereo
  triplets via ISSDC PRADAN; exclude the ~200 GB ck-kernel directory and pull
  only date-matched kernels; mandatory ISRO acknowledgement wording. 8.0/10.

Split discipline: never random-tile-split. The same flagship pits
(Tranquillitatis, Marius Hills, Ingenii) recur across nearly every derived
product; splits must be by physical pit, by site, and by illumination
geometry, with one entire mare basin held out untouched until final testing.

4. INFRASTRUCTURE PLAN (from the VPS guide)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tier 0 (Months 0-9, ~$10-20/month): 2-4 vCPU, 8-16 GB RAM, 200-500 GB disk.
  Runs GDAL, WhiteboxTools, RichDEM, PDAL, scikit-learn, pyshtools. Sufficient
  for everything built on published DTMs — genuinely enough for Paper 1.
Tier 2 (GPU, on demand only, ~$0.30-2.00/hour): prototype on free Colab/
  Kaggle tiers; pay only for final training runs. Never rent monthly.
Tier 1 (Months 12+, hourly/spot, ~$50-150/month-equivalent bursts): 16-32
  DEDICATED cores (not burstable vCPU), 64-128 GB RAM, 1-2 TB NVMe scratch.
  ASP rule of thumb: ~20k x 20k px imagery needs ~40 GB RAM / 16 cores for
  4-20 hours per terrain model; SGM/MGM subprocesses can exceed 4 GB each
  (workable config: 8 processes on 64 GB). Budget 100-250 GB scratch per
  stereo pair; use --keep-only and --resume-at-corr (spot-friendly).
Known traps to engineer around: burstable-vCPU throttling; NFS-killed I/O;
  the 520 GB full ISIS data area (pull base ~26 GB + lro excluding kernels;
  use spiceinit web=yes); and the silent lronaccal IOF/zero-data bug — always
  set RadiometricType=RADIANCE with the SPICE web service.
Total 18-24 month compute budget target: under $600-800, sequenced; snapshot
  the working environment image and destroy idle instances. Apply early for
  cloud research credits and institutional HPC (ASP ships as an HPC module
  with SLURM examples).

5. STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE 0 — FOUNDATION AND REPRODUCTION (Months 0-3)
Step 0.1  Literature lock-down. Build a prior-art matrix over the ~33-item
          reference list already compiled (Wagner & Robinson 2014/2021/2022;
          Le Corre/ESSA 2025; Zhou 2024; Carrer 2024; Kaku 2017; Chappaz
          2017; Zhu 2024; Horvath 2022; Powell 2023; Blair 2017; Theinat
          2020; Chwala 2024; Sauro 2020; Costello 2026; Hurwitz 2013;
          Barker 2015; LunarLeaper concept). One row per paper: data used,
          method, claim, gap left open.
Step 0.2  Stand up Tier 0 box (Ubuntu LTS). Install the terra conda stack
          (gdal, rasterio, whitebox, richdem, pdal, geopandas, scikit-learn,
          xgboost, pyshtools). Snapshot the image.
Step 0.3  Download the index layers first: SHAPEFILE_NAC_DTMS,
          SHAPEFILE_LUNAR_PIT_LOCATIONS, Hurwitz rille shapefile, NAC Stereo
          Catalog. Intersect them: which tube-relevant pits and rille systems
          already have published DTMs, and which have buildable stereo but no
          DTM. This one map defines the whole project's scope.
Step 0.4  Reproduce one published NAC DTM end-to-end (ISIS + ASP) at Mare
          Tranquillitatis Pit on a short-lived Tier 1 rental. Success
          criterion: match the published product within its stated error
          bars. Budget 4-6 weeks; treat as a milestone, not a chore. This
          de-risks the entire photogrammetry arm before it is needed.
Step 0.5  Implement the core primitive on a published DTM: FillDepressions ->
          filled minus original -> depression-depth raster. Verify it
          recovers every catalogued pit inside that DTM footprint.
Gate G0:  Reproduction matches; depression primitive recovers known pits.

PHASE 1 — TERRESTRIAL ANCHOR AND DEGRADATION LADDER (Months 3-9) -> PAPER 1
Step 1.1  Acquire ground truth. Convert the NASA Pits and Caves .f32 clouds
          (King's Bowl, Indian Tunnel surface AND cave interior — the paired
          surface/void case) into LAS/COPC via PDAL. Add Corona/Lanzarote and
          Monte Intraleo open LiDAR where licences permit. If field access
          exists, fly a drone survey over one tube field with an existing
          cave survey (Lava Beds NM, Craters of the Moon, Undara, Kazumura
          class sites) to add a wholly-owned dataset with known geometry.
Step 1.2  Build the reference surface models and register cave centerlines
          to surface coordinates. Ground truth = the vertical projection of
          the surveyed void footprint onto the surface DTM.
Step 1.3  Build the degradation ladder. Resample 2 cm -> 0.5 m -> 2 m ->
          5 m -> 60 m; re-render under a lunar photometric function (Hapke
          BRDF via Blender, PANGU, or ASP's SfS forward model) across a grid
          of sun incidence/azimuth; inject NAC-like MTF blur, noise, and
          pushbroom geometry; strip vegetation returns to mimic regolith.
Step 1.4  Run the depression-depth + anisotropic linear-feature detector
          (high-pass residual -> Hessian/Frangi vesselness tuned to 60-300 m
          widths -> continuity and alignment scoring) at every ladder rung.
Step 1.5  Measure detection probability versus GSD x sun angle x roof
          thickness. This detectability curve answers RQ1 and is Paper 1:
          low risk, guaranteed result, directly informative for future
          mission camera specifications.
Gate G1:  Curve produced with confidence intervals; the GSD at which
          tube signatures vanish is quantified. If the sag signal is already
          below noise at 2-5 m equivalent, pivot Phase 2 toward pit-chain /
          bridge-segment detection instead of pure sag (pre-registered
          contingency).

PHASE 2 — LUNAR ROOF-SAG SEARCH AND CANDIDATE CATALOGUE (Months 9-15)
          -> PAPER 2
Step 2.1  Port the validated detector to all published NAC DTMs in mare
          terrain (Tier 0 workload). Detrend against SLDEM2015 / low-pass
          surfaces; compute morphometric stacks (depth, width, elongation,
          continuity, curvature, wall slope, roughness, alignment).
Step 2.2  Build the confusion layer BEFORE scoring candidates: rasterized
          Hurwitz rilles (offset-corrected against LROC), LU5M812TGT crater
          chains, wrinkle-ridge and graben maps. Any linear-depression claim
          must survive explicit discrimination against all five confusers —
          this discrimination test is the paper's core contribution.
Step 2.3  Prioritize rille systems for bridge/sag search: Rima Sharp, Rima
          Hadley, Rimae Prinz, Schroter's Valley, Vallis Alpes, Marius Hills
          network. Exploit the Hurwitz catalogue's own definition (rilles
          continuously unroofed by construction) — partially roofed segments
          are precisely what it excludes, hence our opportunity.
Step 2.4  Multi-illumination stacking on NAC CDRs over top candidates: treat
          sun azimuth as the pseudo-temporal axis (the lunar analog of
          multi-temporal NDVI decline); photometric-stereo consistency checks
          separate real topography from albedo/SfS artifacts.
Step 2.5  Where a high-value candidate lacks a DTM but the Stereo Catalog
          shows a pair: spin up Tier 1 (spot), build the DTM with asp_mgm,
          snapshot, destroy. Consider Chandrayaan-2 TMC-2 triplets / OHRC
          for sub-metre follow-up at the very best sites (budget several
          weeks of PRADAN/kernel friction).
Step 2.6  Release a candidate catalogue with per-candidate evidence vectors,
          confuser-discrimination scores, and honest nulls.
Gate G2:  Catalogue passes confusion analysis; false-candidate density per
          10^4 km^2 reported.

PHASE 3 — MULTI-EVIDENCE FUSION, PU LEARNING, PHYSICS SCREENING
          (Months 15-24) -> PAPER 3
Step 3.1  Co-register evidence layers to a common frame (SLDEM2015 base):
          Diviner topography-removed nighttime T and rock abundance, Mini-RF
          CPR, GRAIL gradients (Gzz, Ghh from GRGM1200A/GL1500E via
          pyshtools), SELENE LRS echo tracks. Regional scope only: Marius
          Hills + Mare Tranquillitatis + Mare Ingenii.
Step 3.2  Build the hierarchical fusion model across the 2 m -> 30 km scale
          gap: multi-resolution Bayesian model (Pyro/NumPyro) or a graph
          neural network over a nested cell hierarchy. The scale-bridging
          machinery is itself a methods contribution publishable in a remote
          sensing venue independent of the lunar result.
Step 3.3  Handle labels honestly: positive-unlabeled learning (~20 positives,
          no verified negatives), one-class/anomaly baselines, MGC3+HiRISE
          cross-body pretraining with explicit domain-gap reporting, and
          conformal prediction (MAPIE/crepes) for calibrated coverage.
Step 3.4  Physics-informed screening: encode finite-element stability bounds
          (realistic spans ~60-300 m; down-weight geometries like the
          implausible 9 km x 60 km GRAIL candidate) as a prior or
          regularizer, so mechanically impossible candidates are suppressed
          automatically. Use Costello 2026 protolith contrasts
          (competent basalt at Tranquillitatis vs friable vesicular material
          at Marius Hills) as a covariate.
Step 3.5  Optional methods spin-off (Paper 4 track): benchmark 2D CNN vs
          point-cloud-native networks (PointNet++, KPConv, Point Transformer)
          on pit classification, focused on overhang cases where 2.5D
          rasters structurally fail.
Gate G3:  Fusion beats every single-stream baseline on calibrated metrics on
          the held-out basin.

PHASE 4 — TARGET RANKING AND MISSION HANDOFF (Months 24-30)
Step 4.1  Value-of-information ranking: combine P(void) with accessibility
          (slope, boulder abundance from Diviner rock abundance, illumination
          and comms windows) and science return per candidate.
Step 4.2  Automated pit-interior reconstruction with per-vertex error bars
          (multi-illumination photometric stereo + shadow-boundary
          constraints) for the top 3-5 targets, extending Zhou 2024 from
          hand-tuned single sites to an automated pipeline.
Step 4.3  Open release: code, degraded-analog dataset, candidate catalogue,
          fusion model. Approach the LunarLeaper and Moon-Diver communities
          with the ranked list. Benchmark ESSA's shapefiles against ours
          with contamination audited via its published product list.

6. EVALUATION PROTOCOL (non-negotiable)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The base rate is brutal: ~6 x 10^6 km^2 of maria tiled at 5 x 5 km gives
~240,000 tiles against ~20 usable positives; a 1% false-positive rate yields
~2,400 false candidates versus 20 real — precision near 0.8%. Accuracy and
balanced-set F1 conceal this completely. Therefore report:
  - performance on unseen PITS, unseen SITES, unseen ILLUMINATION, unseen
    INSTRUMENTS — never unseen tiles from seen pits;
  - detection probability vs GSD and sun angle (the Phase 1 curve);
  - FALSE POSITIVES PER 10^4 km^2, not FPR;
  - explicit confusion breakdown vs rilles, ridges, graben, crater chains;
  - calibrated probabilities with conformal coverage, never hard detections;
  - cross-body tests (Mars-trained/Moon-tested and reverse);
  - all claims phrased as calibrated inference, never verified detection —
    nothing subsurface on the Moon is verifiable today except the radar-
    evidenced Tranquillitatis conduit.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. RISK REGISTER AND MITIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

R1 No ground-truth validation on the Moon -> design for calibrated
   probability output; anchor all method validation on Earth (Phase 1).
R2 Sag signal below DTM noise floor -> tested on terrestrial data FIRST at
   Gate G1; pre-registered pivot to pit-chain/bridge detection.
R3 Confuser dominance (rilles/ridges/graben/chains) -> confusion layer built
   before candidate scoring; discrimination is the contribution, not a caveat.
R4 SfS albedo-topography confusion manufacturing fake depressions ->
   multi-illumination consistency checks (Step 2.4).
R5 Overclaiming GRAIL/thermal: GRAIL resolves only km-scale deficits; Diviner
   sees the pit, not the cave -> both used strictly as coarse priors or
   confirmers, stated explicitly in every paper.
R6 Stereo-coverage scarcity (~2% of surface) -> regional scope declared up
   front; targeted Tier 1 DTM builds only where the Stereo Catalog permits.
R7 Compute/cost overrun -> tier sequencing, snapshots, spot instances,
   --resume-at-corr; hard budget ceiling ~$800 before external credits.
R8 Prior-art collision with ESSA -> cite early, position as inference-vs-
   detection, audit product-list overlap, use it as the baseline to beat.
R9 Licence exposure -> NASA analog dataset is research-use-only; ISRO
   acknowledgement mandatory; LROC web-product terms distinct from PDS
   public domain; audit before any redistribution.
R10 Label circularity -> pit-level splits, held-out basin, ESSA never used
   as labels.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. BOTTOM LINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The forestry pipeline transfers; the physics does not. The point cloud gives
the roof, never the void. So: prove the method on Earth where the answer is
known; run the cheap analysis on DTMs that already exist; buy cores only when
a specific site demands them; fuse every independent evidence stream at its
honest resolution; report calibrated probabilities against a brutal base rate;
and hand a ranked, physically screened target list to the missions that can
actually go and look. Done in that order, this is two to three solid papers,
an open benchmark the field currently lacks, and a defensible answer to a
question no one has systematically asked: not "where are the holes," but
"where are the roofs still holding."
