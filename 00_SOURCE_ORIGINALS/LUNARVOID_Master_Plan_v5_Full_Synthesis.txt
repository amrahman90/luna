PART I — THE SYNTHESIS
=====================================================================

=====================================================================
1. THE ONE INSIGHT THAT ORGANISES EVERYTHING
=====================================================================

Laying all six analyses side by side produces a conclusion none of them
stated alone:

    THIS PROJECT IS NOT COMPUTE-LIMITED, NOT DATA-LIMITED, AND NOT
    METHOD-LIMITED. IT IS LABEL-LIMITED AND VALIDATION-LIMITED.

  - Compute: a $10-20/month box runs everything through candidate
    generation. The VCI detector ran in ~30 s on a 16 GB laptop.
    Photogrammetry bursts cost a few hundred dollars, total.
  - Data: hundreds of pre-made NAC DTMs at 2-5 m, a global 59 m DEM,
    10 m Kaguya imagery streamable as cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs, and
    0.25 m Chandrayaan-2 coverage. All free.
  - Method: ICP registration, kriged systematic-error correction,
    watershed depression segmentation and VCI are all published with
    working parameters and measured error budgets.
  - Labels: roughly 20 lunar pits plausibly related to lava tubes, out
    of ~281 catalogued. Zero verified negatives anywhere on the Moon.
    One radar-evidenced conduit, at Mare Tranquillitatis.

Every design decision below follows from that asymmetry. Do not spend
the budget on cores. Spend it on manufacturing ground truth where
ground truth can exist — on Earth — and on proving, quantitatively,
that the signal being claimed sits above the measurement noise floor.

A second synthesis-level observation reframes the whole enterprise. The
two DLR papers and the lunar problem share a single structure: RECOVER
A LOW-AMPLITUDE, SPATIALLY COHERENT TOPOGRAPHIC SIGNAL FROM BENEATH AN
OBSCURING SURFACE LAYER. Snow beneath shrub canopy; tree stems beneath
canopy occlusion; a flexed basalt roof beneath regolith mantle. That
framing is more accurate and more fundable than "forestry methods
applied to the Moon," and it is what makes the component inheritance
legitimate rather than decorative.

=====================================================================
2. CORE THESIS
=====================================================================

A tree is above the surface; a lava tube is below it. Photogrammetry
reconstructs only the illuminated outer envelope, so a lunar point
cloud contains zero direct information about a void 20-100 m down.

Therefore: WE DO NOT DETECT LAVA TUBES. WE INFER THEM, WITH CALIBRATED
UNCERTAINTY, AND WE SAY SO.

The one methodological bridge, carried through every version and now
validated by inherited components:

    Forestry:  CHM       = DSM - DTM
    Lunar:     depression-depth raster = sink_filled(DTM) - DTM

This is the exact mathematical mirror of a canopy height model, with
the sign inverted. It is the anchor of the detection method.

The distinctive scientific hook: the field searches for HOLES. We also
search for ROOFS — the shallow, linear, laterally continuous residual
depression produced by flexure of an intact tube roof.

Not "where are the holes." Where are the roofs still holding.

=====================================================================
3. THE EVIDENCE HIERARCHY  (synthesis product — no single source
   in the literature states this as one table)
=====================================================================

Every evidence stream exists as a separate published literature. Their
resolutions differ by four orders of magnitude, and each is routinely
overclaimed. What each can and cannot constrain:

STREAM          | RESOLUTION | CAN CONSTRAIN        | CANNOT CONSTRAIN
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------
NAC DTM         | 2-5 m      | Pit geometry, wall   | Anything below
morphometry     |            | slope, overhangs,    | the surface
                |            | sag amplitude,       |
                |            | lineament continuity |
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------
NAC imagery     | 0.5-2 m    | Shadow-derived depth,| Subsurface
multi-illum.    |            | photometric          | anything
                |            | roughness, artifact  |
                |            | discrimination       |
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------
Chandrayaan-2   | 0.25 m     | Sub-metre pit        | Coverage is
OHRC            |            | interior detail      | sparse, targeted
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------
Kaguya TC       | 10 m /     | Regional context,    | Pit interiors,
+ USGS DTMs     | 35 m DTM   | co-registration      | overhangs
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------
SLDEM2015       | ~59 m      | Trend surface,       | The 60-300 m
                | 3-4 m vert | reference frame      | feature itself
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------
Diviner thermal | ~128 ppd   | PIT presence (pits   | THE CAVE BEHIND
                |            | ~100 K warmer at     | THE PIT. Blind
                |            | night); roof         | to it. Confirmer
                |            | thickness via        | only.
                |            | damping inversion    |
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------
Mini-RF / DFSAR | partial    | Genuine subsurface   | Cannot survey;
radar imaging   | coverage   | echo — the only      | needs radar
                |            | direct cave evidence | expertise
                |            | to date              |
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------
SELENE LRS      | sparse     | Confirm/refute a     | Cannot survey
sounder         | tracks     | specific candidate   |
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------
GRAIL gravity   | 10-30 km   | Kilometre-scale mass | ABSOLUTELY NOT a
                | effective  | deficit only         | 60-300 m tube.
                |            |                      | Do not imply it.
----------------|------------|----------------------|------------------

Two published cautionary results anchor the bottom two rows. Thermal
modelling established that infrared readily detects pit signatures but
is insensitive to the caves behind them. And a GRAIL-derived candidate
geometry of roughly 9 km wide, 60 km long, 605 m deep with 55 m central
height is regarded by structural modellers as implausible as a single
stable void, against finite-element estimates of realistic spans of
roughly 60-300 m beneath observed skylights.

The fusion problem is therefore not "combine the evidence." It is
"bridge four orders of magnitude without letting the coarse streams
manufacture false precision in the fine ones." That scale-bridging
machinery is itself publishable independent of the lunar result.

=====================================================================
4. THE CRITICAL PATH
=====================================================================

Exactly one causal chain determines whether this project produces
anything at all:

    kriged systematic-error correction
              |
              v
    measured noise floor on zero-change panels
              |
              v
    IS ROOF-SAG AMPLITUDE ABOVE THAT FLOOR?
              |
        +-----+-----+
        |           |
       YES          NO
        |           |
    full plan   pre-registered pivot
    proceeds    + publishable null

Everything else is engineering. This is why WP0 and WP1 are not warm-up
phases — they are the project's decision point, and they are also the
cheapest phases. The correct sequencing is to reach the decision before
spending anything substantial.

The DLR inheritance is what makes this measurable rather than a gamble.
Earlier plan versions treated DTM systematic error as an irreducible
noise floor; the snow-depth paper demonstrates a factor-of-six further
reduction AFTER ICP convergence, and demonstrates it with independent
check points.

5. COMPONENTS INHERITED WITH PUBLISHED PARAMETERS
=====================================================================

From Mueller et al. (2026), Arctic Science, doi:10.1139/as-2025-0062,
CC BY 4.0, code at gitlab.com/dlr-dw/uav-snow-depth-monitoring, data at
Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.14608566:

I1  ICP FINE REGISTRATION (CloudCompare 2.13.2)
    RMS convergence threshold 1.0e-7; final overlap 90%; random
    sampling limit 500,000 points; farthest point removal enabled;
    scalar field weights on both clouds; no rotation/translation
    constraints.
    LUNAR CHANGE: disable "adjust scale." Scale is fixed by the camera
    model and SPICE geometry; allowing it absorbs real relief.

I2  KRIGED DISTORTION MAP  [highest-value transfer]
    pykrige, spherical variogram, 2000 x 2000 interpolation grid, fed
    by 205 manually selected no-change points.
    Measured performance at 20 independent check points:
        initial        Z bias +4.18 m, RMSE 4.53 m
        after ICP      Z bias -0.53 m, RMSE 1.26 m
        after kriging  Z bias -0.02 m, RMSE 0.21 m
    Reported property that matters most: the kriged surface was a
    smooth low-frequency gradient contrasting with the high-frequency
    variability of the real signal — the correction removed global
    alignment error without smoothing localised features.
    LUNAR: no-change points on flat mare panels, LOLA track crossings,
    and independently surveyed flat surfaces. Always publish the
    spatial-frequency comparison to prove the signal survived.

I3  ZERO-CHANGE VALIDATION SURFACE
    1.9 million pixels within 1 m buffers of snow-cleared road
    centrelines where true depth was ~0. Full distribution reported:
    mean 10.99 cm, median 8.24 cm, SD 21.64 cm, P10 -5.72, P90 33.17.
    LUNAR: flat mare panels with no mapped features. This distribution
    IS the noise floor and IS the Gate G1 criterion.

I4  WATERSHED DEPRESSION SEGMENTATION (Orfeo ToolBox 8.1.2)
    Depth threshold 0.01; flood level 0.1; vector mode; tile 4096 px;
    polygon stitching enabled; minimum object 1000 px. Post-processing:
    area filter 15-4000 m^2; Douglas-Peucker simplification at 1 m.
    Achieved 291 polygons against 269 reference, median IoU 0.630,
    precision 0.834, recall 0.653, F1 0.733.
    LUNAR CHANGE: do not invert the DSM. Pits and sag are already
    basins. Rescale the area filter to lunar feature sizes.

I5  SEGMENT-AND-BUFFER WITH DIRECTIONAL SECTORS
    Buildings 1 m exclusion + 5 m analysis; roads 4 m + 5 m, segmented
    at max 50 m / min 12 m, classified N/S/E/W. Found South-facing
    embankments significantly deeper (0.77 m) than North (0.66 m), East
    and West (0.60 m each), Mann-Whitney U with Bonferroni, p < 0.001.
    LUNAR: buffer and segment along rille centrelines and candidate
    lineaments; stratify residual depth BY SUN-AZIMUTH SECTOR. If
    apparent depth varies with illumination azimuth, the feature is a
    shading artifact. This is the concrete test for shape-from-shading
    confusion that earlier plan versions listed as an untested risk.

I6  DAMPING-DEPTH THERMAL WORKFLOW (their Appendix C)
    n_f = exp(-S_eff / D);  D = sqrt(2*kappa/omega);
    kappa = k_eff/(rho*c_p);  FDD_s = n_f * FDD_a;
    dT_s = (FDD_a - FDD_s)/(N_f + N_t).
    D = sqrt(2*kappa/omega) is the standard diffusion skin depth, so
    the PHYSICS is not novel — planetary thermal modelling already uses
    it. What transfers is the WORKFLOW PATTERN: remotely-sensed
    thickness map -> damping factor -> predicted temperature-offset map
    -> comparison against measurement. Run inverse, a measured Diviner
    nighttime anomaly becomes a constraint on the material column above
    a void.

I7  CONSERVATIVE LOWER-BOUND FRAMING
    Their snow-free reference captured shrub canopy rather than ground
    (bias -17.32 cm, RMSE 30.45 cm, R^2 0.35; improving to -6.7 cm,
    20.7 cm, R^2 0.46 with high-shrub sites removed). They reframed the
    product as a defensible MINIMUM snow depth rather than calling it
    failure.
    LUNAR: regolith mantles and smooths the true structural roof, so
    measured sag is a LOWER BOUND on true flexure. Adopt verbatim.

From Reichenzeller et al. (2026), Forests 17:807,
doi:10.3390/f17070807, CC BY:

I8  VERTICAL COMPLEXITY INDEX AS AN OVERHANG DETECTOR
    VCI = -sum(p_i * ln p_i) / ln(n_HB), the Shannon evenness of the
    vertical point distribution in a voxel column. Pipeline: VCI raster
    -> threshold -> local maximum filter (neighbourhood 5) -> cell
    centroids.
    Over flat mare a photogrammetric cloud is effectively 2.5D and VCI
    collapses toward 0. At a PIT WALL OR OVERHANG the cloud is
    genuinely 3D within one column, so VCI spikes exactly where rasters
    structurally fail. Tree stem and pit wall are the same geometry.
    Calibrated terrestrial set: 3-15 m height segment, 0.5 m pixel, 1 m
    height bin, threshold 0.4 -> 75.25% of overstory trees detected,
    precision 0.8172, F1 0.7835.
    Lunar translation: depth band scaled to pit depth (e.g. 5-60 m);
    pixel 2-5 m; height bin 2-5 m; threshold RE-CALIBRATED per I9.
    Ran in ~30 s per dataset on an Intel Core i7-10810U with 16 GB RAM.

I9  THRESHOLDS DO NOT TRANSFER ACROSS INSTRUMENTS
    Applying the SfM-tuned set to UAV-LiDAR gave recall 0.9727 but
    precision 0.5297 (95 false positives), F1 0.6859. Re-tuning ONE
    parameter — VCI threshold 0.4 -> 0.8 — produced F1 0.86, recall
    0.89, precision 0.82, exceeding every SfM result.
    CONSEQUENCE: the degradation ladder must re-tune per resolution
    rung and per instrument. A fixed threshold across GSDs would
    manufacture an artificial detectability cliff. Report the threshold
    trajectory as a result.

I10 APPARENT FALSE POSITIVES MAY BE UNLABELLED TRUE POSITIVES
    Of 34 apparent FPs, 21 matched documented understory trees, leaving
    13 genuine errors and lifting precision above 0.93.
    CONSEQUENCE: with ~20 lunar labels and no verified negatives, every
    apparent FP must be inspected in NAC imagery before scoring.
    Published deep-learning work surfaced two previously uncatalogued
    skylights while surveying only ~1.9% of the maria — blind scoring
    against the Pit Atlas systematically understates precision.

I11 STRATIFIED DETECTABILITY WITH SIGNIFICANCE TESTING
    Detection stratified by diameter at breast height, Wilcoxon
    rank-sum at alpha 0.01: trees below 0.25 m particularly hard;
    ~81% detected at DBH >= 0.2 m, ~91% at >= 0.6 m. This IS a
    detectability curve and is the template for our headline product.

I12 INDEPENDENT CONFOUND COVARIATE, WITH NULLS REPORTED
    Laser Penetration Index computed from an INDEPENDENT airborne laser
    dataset specifically so it would not share data gaps with the cloud
    under test; plus distance-to-nearest-canopy-gap as a second check.
    LPI differences significant at alpha 0.01; canopy-gap distance NOT
    significant at alpha 0.05 — and the null was reported.
    LUNAR: LOLA track density, NAC image count per tile, SLDEM2015
    slope. Report nulls.

I13 SENSITIVITY HEATMAP AS DELIVERABLE
    96 parameter combinations, F1 spanning only 0.6971 to 0.7835 — the
    narrow spread is itself the robustness evidence. Template for the
    benchmark ablation tables.

I14 PREDICTABLE GEOMETRIC FAILURE MODE
    Only 4-6 of 11 documented leaning stems detected, because a leaning
    trunk splits across voxel columns and flattens the vertical
    distribution.
    LUNAR PREDICTION, pre-registered: FUNNEL-shaped pits will degrade
    VCI identically. Mare Tranquillitatis Pit has a funnel plus a
    narrower inner pit; Marius Hills Hole sits in friable vesicular
    material with sloped walls. Expect best performance on vertical
    inner walls, worst on funnel slopes — then test it.

I15 CALIBRATE-ONCE-THEN-TRANSFER-UNCHANGED, and DECLARED MATCHING
    RADIUS. They calibrated on one dataset and applied it unchanged
    elsewhere, and justified a 1.5 m matching radius explicitly while
    noting no community standard exists.
    LUNAR: calibrate on Tranquillitatis, transfer unchanged to Marius
    Hills and Ingenii. Declare matching radius >= 30 m to respect Pit
    Atlas positional accuracy, and report sensitivity to that choice.

=====================================================================
9. EVALUATION PROTOCOL  (non-negotiable)
=====================================================================

THE BASE RATE. Maria at ~16% of a 3.79e7 km^2 surface is ~6e6 km^2;
tiled at 5 x 5 km that is ~240,000 candidate tiles against ~20 usable
positives. A 1% false-positive rate returns ~2,400 false candidates
against 20 real ones — precision near 0.8%. Accuracy and balanced-set
F1 conceal this entirely. (Order-of-magnitude; the exact tiling shifts
the numbers, not the conclusion.)

MANDATORY REPORTING
  - Splits by physical PIT, SITE, ILLUMINATION and INSTRUMENT — never
    by tile. NAC images overlap; DTMs derive from overlapping pairs;
    flagship pits recur across nearly every derived product.
  - One entire mare basin held out untouched until final evaluation.
  - Detection probability vs GSD and sun angle (the WP1 curve).
  - FALSE POSITIVES PER 10^4 km^2, not false-positive rate.
  - Every apparent FP manually inspected before scoring, with a
    separate "unlabelled candidate" class reported (I10).
  - Matching radius >= 30 m, declared, with sensitivity reported (I15).
  - Detection stratified by feature size with Wilcoxon / Kruskal-Wallis
    significance testing (I11).
  - Confound covariates from independent datasets, nulls reported(I12).
  - Threshold trajectory per instrument and resolution (I9).
  - Zero-change panel residuals in full — mean, median, SD, P10, P90.
  - Conformal coverage, never hard detections.
  - Cross-body tests, Mars-trained/Moon-tested and reverse.
  - All claims phrased as calibrated inference. Nothing subsurface on
    the Moon is verifiable today except the radar-evidenced
    Tranquillitatis conduit.

10. RISK REGISTER
=====================================================================

R1  No lunar ground truth -> calibrated probabilities only; all method
    validation anchored on Earth (WP1).
R2  Sag below the noise floor -> now MEASURED, not assumed, via I2+I3
    at Gate G1. Pre-registered pivot below.
R3  Confuser dominance -> confusion layer built before scoring; I5
    azimuth test for shading artifacts.
R4  SfS albedo/topography confusion -> multi-illumination consistency
    plus I5.
R5  Overclaiming GRAIL or thermal -> constrained by written policy in
    every output, per the Section 3 hierarchy.
R6  Stereo scarcity (~2%) -> regional scope declared up front.
R7  Compute overrun -> tier sequencing, snapshots, spot instances.
R8  Prior-art collision with ESSA -> cite early, position as inference
    vs detection, audit product-list overlap.
R9  Label circularity -> pit-level splits; ESSA never used as labels.
R10 Licence exposure -> per-dataset audit before redistribution.
R11 Scope creep -> WP7 partner-gated; exclusions enforced at gates.
R12 Threshold overfitting across resolutions -> I9 protocol.

DELIBERATE EXCLUSIONS (stated because reviewers reward it): no global
detection claim; no gravity inversion for sub-kilometre geometry; no
in-house robotic hardware; no mineral or ice classification without
matched spectral ground truth; no ISRU or life-support engineering; no
claim of verified subsurface detection beyond existing radar evidence.

=====================================================================
11. THE NULL-RESULT PLAN
=====================================================================

If Gate G1 shows roof-sag amplitude sits below the corrected noise
floor at 2-5 m posting, the project does not fail — it pivots, and the
null is itself publishable:

  PUBLISH: "Detectability limits for intact lava tube roof signatures
  in orbital topography," reporting the corrected noise floor, the
  degradation curve, and the minimum GSD and roof-thickness combination
  at which sag becomes recoverable. This directly specifies camera and
  altimeter requirements for future missions. LLTB-1 ships regardless.

  PIVOT TO: pit-chain and bridge-segment detection, where the signal is
  larger; VCI-based overhang characterisation at known pits, which does
  not depend on sag at all; and the WP3 fusion work, which stands on
  existing catalogued pits.

Pre-registering this now is what makes Gate G1 an honest test rather
than a result to be argued around later.

=====================================================================
13. BOTTOM LINE
=====================================================================

The forestry pipeline transfers. The physics does not. The point cloud
gives the roof, never the void.

But the machinery for pulling a low-amplitude, spatially coherent
signal out from beneath an obscuring layer — and for proving honestly
that you did not invent it — now transfers almost component for
component, with published parameters and measured error budgets.

So: measure the noise floor before believing any signal. Prove the
method on Earth where the answer is known. Publish the benchmark the
field lacks. Buy cores only when a specific site demands them. Fuse
every evidence stream at its honest resolution and no finer. Report
against a brutal base rate. Inspect every false positive before calling
it one. And hand a ranked, physically screened target list to the
missions that can actually go and look.

Not "where are the holes." Where are the roofs still holding.
