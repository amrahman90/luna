=====================================================================
0. WHAT CHANGED IN v3.0, AND WHY
=====================================================================

v2.0 was written to sound fundable. v1.0 was written to be executable.
v3.0 keeps v2.0's ambition and structure while removing the claims a
planetary-science reviewer would reject on physical grounds.

The single hardest constraint, which v2.0 does not acknowledge anywhere,
is this: THERE IS NO DIRECT OBSERVATION OF THE INTERIOR OF ANY INTACT
LUNAR LAVA TUBE. One radar dataset indicates an accessible conduit
behind the Mare Tranquillitatis Pit. That is the entirety of the
subsurface ground truth for the Moon. Approximately 20 pits in mare and
highland terrain are plausibly tube-related, out of ~281 catalogued.
There are zero verified negatives. Roughly 2% of the surface has
high-resolution stereo coverage.

Every v2.0 component was triaged against that reality:

ADOPTED (genuine improvements, incorporated in full)
  - Work-package structure. Improves legibility and fundability.
  - The benchmark dataset (LLTB-1). PROMOTED TO FLAGSHIP DELIVERABLE.
    This is the strongest single idea in v2.0 and directly completes
    the degradation-ladder work already planned in v1.0.
  - Decision support / mission target ranking (WP6). Already present in
    v1.0 as a value-of-information layer; v2.0's framing is better.
  - Strategic per-venue publication planning.
  - Explicit ablation, blind testing, domain adaptation, uncertainty
    estimation as named validation activities.
  - Project naming and positioning ambition.

REFRAMED (good instinct, unsupportable as stated)
  - "Digital Twin" -> "Uncertainty-Quantified Geospatial Model (UQGM)".
    A digital twin is a model synchronised to a real observed system.
    You cannot twin an unobserved void. We build honest twins only
    where observation exists: terrestrial analog caves (full LiDAR
    ground truth) and lunar pit interiors down to the shadow line
    (multi-illumination photometric stereo). Everything deeper is a
    SIMULATION ENSEMBLE with error bars, and is labelled as such.
  - "Global Lunar Lava Tube Database" -> "Regional Candidate Catalogue
    with Calibrated Probabilities and Explicit Coverage Bounds".
    Global detection is impossible at 2% stereo coverage. Claiming it
    invites immediate rejection.
  - "Knowledge Graph" -> "Evidence-Provenance Graph". Retained, but its
    job is tracking which instrument, at which resolution, with which
    uncertainty, supports each candidate — not ontological reasoning
    over minerals we have not measured. This version is actually
    useful for multi-scale fusion.
  - "Roof thickness estimation" -> retained ONLY at pits where wall
    cross-sections are visible (e.g. ~26 m at Marius Hills Hole).
    Generalised roof-thickness mapping is not derivable from available
    data.
  - Multi-agent robotics -> moved to WP7 as a PARTNER-DEPENDENT
    extension with a defined interface, not a core deliverable.

REJECTED (would fail technical review)
  - "Gravity inversion" to reconstruct tube geometry. GRAIL's effective
    resolution is 10-30 km; candidate tubes are 60-300 m wide. This is
    a 2-3 order-of-magnitude mismatch. Exactly this error produced a
    published candidate ~9 km wide, 60 km long, 605 m deep with 55 m
    central height — a geometry structural modellers regard as
    implausible as a single stable void. GRAIL is retained strictly as
    a coarse km-scale mass-deficit prior.
  - Ice detection inside mare lava tubes. LAMP and volatile datasets
    address polar cold traps; candidate tubes are equatorial mare
    features. Wrong latitude band, wrong physics.
  - ShadowCam as a detection input. It images permanently shadowed
    polar regions; mare tubes are not in PSRs. Retained only as an
    illumination-limits reference.
  - NeRF / Gaussian Splatting as a primary reconstruction method for
    orbital data. Both assume photometric consistency across many
    views. Lunar surface albedo, extreme illumination variation and
    sparse view geometry break that assumption. Retained as a
    candidate method for TERRESTRIAL analog reconstruction only,
    benchmarked against ASP stereo rather than assumed superior.
  - Reinforcement learning for mission planning. No validated
    environment exists to train in; an RL policy trained on a
    simulator we ourselves built is circular.
  - "Interactive LLM Assistant" as a research contribution. It is an
    interface, not a result.
  - The v2.0 self-scorecard (e.g. "Digital Twin 3/10 -> 10/10"). Adding
    components a team cannot execute LOWERS funding competitiveness.
    Reviewers score feasibility, not component count.

NET EFFECT: fewer promises, all of them defensible; one flagship
deliverable (LLTB-1) the field genuinely lacks; and an explicit
scope-discipline section, which reads as maturity rather than
limitation.

=====================================================================
1. VISION AND PRINCIPAL GOAL
=====================================================================

Develop and validate a calibrated, uncertainty-quantified inference
framework that fuses orbital morphometry, thermal, radar and gravity
evidence to identify and rank lunar lava tube candidates; establish the
first open benchmark for planetary void detection; and deliver
mission-ready target rankings to robotic cave-exploration programmes.

Guiding principle: WE DO NOT DETECT LAVA TUBES. WE INFER THEM, WITH
ERROR BARS, AND WE SAY SO. A tree is above the surface; a lava tube is
below it. Photogrammetry reconstructs only the illuminated envelope, so
every optical conclusion is indirect inference from surface expression.

Central methodological insight carried from v1.0: replace the forestry
canopy height model (CHM = DSM - DTM) with a DEPRESSION-DEPTH RASTER
(sink-filled DTM minus DTM). This is its exact mathematical mirror and
is the anchor of the detection method.

Distinctive scientific hook: the field searches for HOLES. We also
search for ROOFS — the shallow, linear, laterally continuous residual
depression produced by flexure of an intact tube roof.

=====================================================================
2. RESEARCH QUESTIONS
=====================================================================

RQ1  At what ground sample distance, sun geometry and roof thickness
     does a lava tube's surface expression become undetectable?
RQ2  Can intact-roof sag be discriminated from sinuous rilles, wrinkle
     ridges, graben, lava channels and secondary crater chains at 2-5 m
     DTM posting?
RQ3  Does hierarchical fusion of morphometric, thermal, radar and
     gravity evidence across a 2 m-to-30 km scale gap yield
     better-calibrated void probabilities than any single stream?
RQ4  How should candidate uncertainty be represented so that mission
     planners can act on it, and which targets maximise expected
     information gain per unit mission cost?
RQ5  Can uncertainty-quantified geospatial models of pit interiors and
     analog caves support quantitative habitat and traversability
     assessment?

=====================================================================
3. WORK PACKAGES
=====================================================================

WP0 — FOUNDATION AND REPRODUCIBILITY (M0-M3)
Tasks: prior-art matrix over the ~33-reference corpus; Tier-0
infrastructure and environment snapshot; download index layers
(SHAPEFILE_NAC_DTMS with relative-linear-error and triangulation-RMS
fields, Lunar Pit Atlas, Hurwitz 195-rille shapefile, NAC Stereo
Catalog) and intersect them to define scope; reproduce one published
LROC NAC DTM end-to-end with ISIS + Ames Stereo Pipeline at Mare
Tranquillitatis Pit to within its stated error bars.
Deliverable: reproducibility report; scope map; working depression-depth
primitive verified against catalogued pits.

WP1 — LLTB-1 BENCHMARK AND DETECTABILITY LIMITS (M3-M9) [FLAGSHIP]
Tasks: convert NASA Planetary Pits and Caves analog LiDAR (King's Bowl
530 MB; Indian Tunnel surface 1.1 GB and cave interior 2.07 GB; Fieg;
Sheepridge — research/academic licence, ~4.5 GB total) from .f32 to
LAS/COPC; add Corona/Lanzarote TLS and Grotta di Monte Intraleo open
datasets; optionally fly a drone survey over a tube field with an
existing cave survey. Register surveyed void footprints to surface
DTMs. Build the degradation ladder: 2 cm -> 0.5 m -> 2 m -> 5 m -> 60 m,
re-rendered under a Hapke photometric function across a sun
incidence/azimuth grid, with NAC-like MTF blur, noise and pushbroom
geometry, vegetation returns stripped.
Deliverable: LLTB-1 — the Lunar Lava Tube Benchmark. Paired
surface/subsurface geometry, multi-resolution, multi-illumination, with
true labels, standardised splits, baseline scores and an evaluation
harness. This is the deliverable the field most conspicuously lacks and
the one most likely to be widely cited.
Also: the DETECTABILITY CURVE (detection probability vs GSD x sun angle
x roof thickness), which directly informs future mission camera specs.

WP2 — ROOF-SAG SEARCH AND CANDIDATE CATALOGUE (M9-M15)
Tasks: port the LLTB-1-validated detector to all published NAC DTMs in
mare terrain; detrend against SLDEM2015 (+/-60 deg, ~59 m/px, 3-4 m
vertical); anisotropic Hessian/Frangi vesselness tuned to 60-300 m
widths; continuity and alignment scoring. Build the CONFUSION LAYER
FIRST — rasterised Hurwitz rilles (offset-corrected, since they were
digitised on Kaguya TC), LU5M812TGT craters (~5M to 0.4 km), wrinkle
ridges, graben. Exploit the Hurwitz catalogue's own definition: rilles
are included only where continuously unroofed, so partially roofed
bridge segments are precisely what it excludes. Multi-illumination
stacking on NAC CDRs, treating sun azimuth as the pseudo-temporal axis.
Targeted Tier-1 DTM builds only where the Stereo Catalog permits.
Deliverable: regional candidate catalogue with per-candidate evidence
vectors, confuser-discrimination scores, explicit coverage bounds and
honest nulls.

WP3 — MULTI-EVIDENCE FUSION AND CALIBRATION (M15-M24)
Tasks: co-register evidence layers to SLDEM2015 — Diviner
topography-removed nighttime temperature and rock abundance, Mini-RF /
DFSAR circular polarisation, GRAIL gradients (Gzz, Ghh from
GRGM1200A/GL1500E via pyshtools), SELENE LRS echo tracks. Build the
hierarchical multi-resolution model (Pyro/NumPyro Bayesian, or a GNN
over nested cells) that bridges 2 m to 30 km. Positive-unlabeled
learning over ~20 positives with no verified negatives; one-class and
anomaly baselines; MGC3 + HiRISE cross-body pretraining with explicit
domain-gap reporting; conformal prediction (MAPIE/crepes) for calibrated
coverage. Physics-informed screening encoding finite-element stability
bounds (~60-300 m realistic spans) as a prior, with Costello-type
protolith contrasts (competent basalt at Tranquillitatis vs friable
vesicular material at Marius Hills) as a covariate.
Scope: Marius Hills + Mare Tranquillitatis + Mare Ingenii.
Deliverable: calibrated P(void) maps with coverage guarantees; the
scale-bridging machinery is independently publishable in a remote
sensing venue.

WP4 — UNCERTAINTY-QUANTIFIED GEOSPATIAL MODELS (M18-M27)
Tasks: automated pit-interior reconstruction with per-vertex error bars
using multi-illumination photometric stereo plus shadow-boundary
constraints, extending single-site photogrammetric work into a pipeline.
Full-geometry UQGMs for terrestrial analog caves where LiDAR ground
truth exists. For unobserved lunar interiors, produce SIMULATION
ENSEMBLES (thermal, radiation, structural) spanning the plausible
geometry space rather than a single false-precision model. Thermal
boundary conditions anchored on published values: pits ~100 K warmer
than surroundings at night; modelled cave interiors near a constant
~290 K. Explicitly label every layer as OBSERVED, INFERRED or SIMULATED.
Deliverable: UQGM platform; per-site geometry with error bars;
simulation ensembles with stated assumptions.

WP5 — EVIDENCE-PROVENANCE GRAPH AND GEOLOGICAL CONTEXT (M20-M30)
Tasks: build a graph linking candidate -> evidence -> instrument ->
resolution -> uncertainty -> derived claim, enabling automated audit of
which conclusions rest on which measurements. Attach geological context
(host terrain, protolith competence, rille association, unit age) from
published catalogues. Vision-transformer / segmentation models applied
ONLY to tasks with real labels: pit-wall stratigraphy, boulder and rock
abundance, collapse morphology. No mineral or ice classification claims
without matched spectral ground truth.
Deliverable: queryable provenance graph; per-claim traceability.

WP6 — MISSION DECISION SUPPORT (M24-M30)
Tasks: value-of-information ranking combining P(void) with accessibility
(slope, boulder abundance, illumination and comms windows), descent
corridor and anchor-point analysis from WP4 geometry, and science return.
Habitat-suitability scoring built strictly on quantities we can defend:
radiation shielding from measured roof thickness where visible, thermal
stability from Diviner-anchored models, structural plausibility from
stability bounds. Every score reported as a distribution.
Deliverable: ranked target list with uncertainty; handoff package for
LunarLeaper- and Moon-Diver-class mission teams.

WP7 — ROBOTIC EXPLORATION INTERFACE (PARTNER-DEPENDENT, M27+)
Not a core deliverable of this project. We define and publish the
INTERFACE: LLTB-1-derived analog environments for SLAM and traversability
benchmarking, UQGM-derived descent corridors, and uncertainty-aware
waypoint specifications. Multi-agent architectures, tether systems and
swarm hardware are contributed by robotics partners (e.g. DFKI, TU Delft,
university robotics groups already publishing lava-tube SLAM datasets)
rather than built in-house.
Deliverable: interface specification; joint benchmark with one partner.

=====================================================================
4. METHOD STACK
=====================================================================

Photogrammetry: USGS ISIS3/ISIS8 (lronac2isis, spiceinit web=yes,
lronaccal RadiometricType=RADIANCE — the IOF default silently produces
zero-filled cubes with the SPICE web service), NASA Ames Stereo Pipeline
(bundle_adjust, parallel_stereo --stereo-algorithm asp_mgm, point2dem),
shape-from-shading refinement with albedo-artifact controls.
Terrain: WhiteboxTools FillDepressions, RichDEM, GDAL, SAGA, geomorphons.
Point clouds: PDAL, CloudCompare, Open3D.
Learning: PyTorch; 2D baselines (Mask R-CNN class) benchmarked against
point-cloud-native networks (PointNet++, KPConv, Point Transformer v3),
with explicit focus on overhang cases where 2.5D rasters structurally
fail. Conformal prediction for calibration. PU-learning estimators.
Fusion: Pyro/NumPyro hierarchical Bayesian; GNN over multi-scale cells.
Gravity: pyshtools spherical-harmonic gradients.
Rendering: Hapke BRDF via ASP SfS forward model, PANGU, or Blender.

=====================================================================
5. DATA AND LICENCE DISCIPLINE
=====================================================================

Substrate: LROC NAC DTMs (2-5 m, quarterly releases), filtered by
shapefile error metrics. Imagery: NAC EDR/CDR fetched by product ID only
— the archive is ~579 TB EDR and >1 PB CDR and must never be mirrored.
Trend surface: SLDEM2015. Mid-scale: Kaguya TC USGS/ASP DTMs (~35 m,
LOLA-aligned) as Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs, enabling windowed HTTP reads
that collapse storage requirements. Labels: Lunar Pit Atlas, restricted
to the ~20 mare/highland pits. Hard negatives: Hurwitz rilles,
LU5M812TGT. Transfer: MGC3 + HiRISE. High-resolution follow-up:
Chandrayaan-2 OHRC (0.25 m GSD) and TMC-2 stereo triplets — exclude the
~200 GB attitude-kernel directory, pull date-matched kernels only.
Baseline: published ESSA weights and detection shapefiles, used as a
baseline to beat and never as a label source (it was trained on the Pit
Atlas; scoring it against the Atlas is circular).

Licence audit, not assumption: PDS holdings are public domain but not
analysis-ready; the NASA analog dataset is research/academic use only
with commercial use requiring written consent; ISRO mandates prescribed
acknowledgement wording; LROC web-product terms differ from PDS.

=====================================================================
6. VALIDATION AND EVALUATION PROTOCOL
=====================================================================

The base rate is unforgiving. Maria at ~16% of a 3.79e7 km^2 surface,
tiled at 5 x 5 km, gives ~240,000 candidate tiles against ~20 usable
positives. A 1% false-positive rate returns ~2,400 false candidates
against 20 real ones — precision near 0.8%. Accuracy and balanced-set F1
conceal this entirely.

Mandatory reporting:
  - performance on unseen PITS, SITES, ILLUMINATION and INSTRUMENTS —
    never unseen tiles from seen pits (NAC images overlap; DTMs derive
    from overlapping pairs; flagship pits recur across nearly every
    derived product);
  - one entire mare basin held out untouched until final evaluation;
  - detection probability vs GSD and sun angle (the WP1 curve);
  - FALSE POSITIVES PER 10^4 km^2, not false-positive rate;
  - explicit confusion breakdown against all five confuser classes;
  - conformal coverage rather than hard detections;
  - cross-body tests (Mars-trained/Moon-tested and reverse);
  - ablation over every evidence stream in the fusion model;
  - blind test on a partner-withheld region where feasible.

Validation levels: (1) terrestrial ground truth — the only true
validation available; (2) reproduction of published lunar products;
(3) simulation-based consistency; (4) cross-body transfer; (5) expert
geological review; (6) statistical calibration testing.

=====================================================================
7. SCOPE DISCIPLINE — WHAT THIS PROJECT DELIBERATELY EXCLUDES
=====================================================================

Stated explicitly, because reviewers reward it: no global detection
claim; no gravity inversion for sub-kilometre geometry; no in-house
robotic hardware; no mineral or ice classification without matched
spectral ground truth; no ISRU or life-support engineering; no claim of
verified subsurface detection anywhere except where independent radar
evidence already exists. Each exclusion has a physical or resource
justification recorded in Section 0.

=====================================================================
8. RISK REGISTER
=====================================================================

R1  No lunar ground truth -> calibrated probabilities only; all method
    validation anchored on Earth (WP1).
R2  Sag signal below DTM noise -> tested on LLTB-1 BEFORE lunar work;
    pre-registered pivot to pit-chain and bridge-segment detection.
R3  Confuser dominance -> confusion layer built before scoring.
R4  SfS albedo/topography confusion manufacturing false depressions ->
    multi-illumination consistency checks.
R5  Overclaiming GRAIL or thermal -> both constrained by written policy
    in every output: GRAIL is km-scale only; thermal IR sees the pit,
    not the cave.
R6  Stereo scarcity (~2%) -> regional scope declared up front.
R7  Compute cost -> tiered infrastructure: Tier 0 (~$10-20/month) for
    all of WP0-WP2; GPU hourly on demand; Tier 1 (16-32 dedicated cores,
    64-128 GB RAM, 1-2 TB NVMe) only in bursts, using --resume-at-corr
    for spot instances. Ceiling ~$800 self-funded before credits.
R8  Prior-art collision with ESSA -> cite early, position as inference
    vs detection, audit product-list overlap.
R9  Label circularity -> pit-level splits; ESSA never used as labels.
R10 Licence exposure -> per-dataset audit before any redistribution.
R11 Scope creep (the failure mode of v2.0) -> WP7 partner-gated;
    Section 7 exclusions enforced at each stage gate.

10. PUBLICATION STRATEGY
=====================================================================

1. Remote Sensing of Environment / ISPRS Journal — LLTB-1 and the
   detectability curve (benchmark papers are durable and heavily cited).
2. Icarus or Planetary and Space Science — roof-sag candidate catalogue.
3. IEEE TGRS — hierarchical multi-scale fusion across a 10^4 scale gap
   (methodologically novel independent of the lunar application).
4. JGR Planets — uncertainty-quantified pit-interior models.
5. Acta Astronautica — mission target ranking and habitat scoring.
6. IEEE RA-L — joint robotics benchmark with WP7 partner.
Nature Astronomy remains aspirational and should be attempted only if
WP3 produces a genuinely surprising, independently corroborated result.

=====================================================================
11. BOTTOM LINE
=====================================================================

v2.0's instinct was right: this should be a platform, not a study. But a
platform built on unsupportable physics collapses under review. v3.0
keeps every genuinely strong v2.0 idea — work packages, the benchmark,
decision support, strategic publishing — while replacing gravity
inversion, global claims and twins of unobserved voids with calibrated
inference, explicit coverage bounds and simulation ensembles.

The forestry pipeline transfers; the physics does not. The point cloud
gives the roof, never the void. Prove the method on Earth where the
answer is known, publish the benchmark the field lacks, fuse every
evidence stream at its honest resolution, report against a brutal base
rate, and hand a ranked, physically screened target list to the missions
that can actually go and look.

Not "where are the holes." Where are the roofs still holding.
