## Regressions (reported as-is — no re-tuning)

- **2 m rung, sensor-only: 0.254 → 0.122** (SNR50: 0.069, SNR200:
  0.074). The SNR ordering is non-monotone on n_void = 53; treat the
  ~halved F1 as real, the SNR-resolved ordering as small-set noise.
- Composition at 2 m: 0.115 → 0.109 (additive at the shadow base rate).
- 5 m rung: sensor-only 0.175 vs baseline 0.154 — n_void = 8, no
  statistic; consistent with 13.3 (nil effect at 5 m).

  arm by protocol — documented finding, not rescued.
