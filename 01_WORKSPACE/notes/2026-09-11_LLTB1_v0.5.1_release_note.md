# LLTB-1 v0.5.1 — Connected-component filter wiring (capability release, default off)

**Date:** 2026-09-11
**Supersedes:** `notes/2026-08-22_LLTB1_v0.5_release_note.md`
**Release class:** behaviour-identical capability release (LLTB-1 release
protocol). The evaluation artifacts were authored under the working name
"v0.6 candidate (task C3)" — see
`data/outputs/wp1_detector/cc_filter_evaluation_v0_6.json` and
`code/wp1_detector/cc_filter_eval_v0_6.py`; released as **v0.5.1** because
the default code path is identical to v0.5.
**Headline:** the standalone connected-component engine
(`connected_component_filter.py`) is wired into `sag_detect.py` as
`--cc-filter {off,on,auto}` with per-rung audit fields. Default is **off**
(v0.5 parity) by pre-registered decision rule: `auto` is a no-op at 6/7
sites and −0.0028 F1 at the 7th. The v0.2-era "documentation, not lift"
verdict on component filtering is confirmed on the full 7-site battery.
**Claim discipline:** benchmark of a detector chain on terrestrial analogs;
calibrated inference, never verified detection.

## What changed

### 1. CLI flag: `--cc-filter {off,on,auto}` (+ `--cc-area-min`)

- **off (default)**: the legacy inline scalar `--min-component 5` path,
  bit-for-bit the v0.5 behaviour. `--min-component` is honoured verbatim
  in off mode and acts as the fallback area when `on` is set without
  `--cc-area-min`.
- **on**: uniform minimum component area (cells) via `--cc-area-min`
  through the standalone engine.
- **auto**: the standalone `connected_component_filter` engine with the
  per-rung `AREA_MIN` table (below).

### 2. Per-rung `AREA_MIN` table (`auto` mode)

Authoritative source: `V0.2_PLAN.md` §4.1; scale-awareness rationale
((GSD ratio)²) documented in `connected_component_filter.AREA_MIN_PER_RUNG`.

| Rung (m) | 0.5 | 1 | 2 | 5 | 8 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AREA_MIN (cells) | 50 | 20 | 8 | 3 | 2 | 2 |

### 3. New per-rung audit fields

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `cc_filter` | mode used (`off`/`on`/`auto`) |
| `cc_area_min_effective` | area minimum actually applied (legacy scalar in off; table value in auto) |
| `cc_components_total` / `cc_components_kept` / `cc_components_dropped` | component counts through the filter |
| `cc_components_dropped_ratio` | dropped / total |
| `cc_cells_dropped` | cells removed (auto/on engine path) |

### 4. Default-path parity with v0.5 (verification)

- **7/7 site F1 parity** vs the frozen v0.4 §5 site table (frozen through
  v0.5), tolerance 2e-3 (pins are 4-dp rounded): `parity.all_ok = true`,
  zero failures.
- Off-mode mask equality with the legacy
  `filter_small_components(min_component)` path is asserted exactly
  (`np.array_equal`) in `test_cc_filter_modes.py::test_off_mode_is_legacy_parity`.
- Smoke test unchanged and passing: per-rung F1 0.391608…/0.000/0.800
  (full-precision pin 0.39160839160839167, tol 1e-6), fusion AUC 0.990.
- pytest: **73 passed** (58 prior + 15 new mode tests in
  `code/tests/test_cc_filter_modes.py`), re-verified 2026-09-11.
- Frozen Paper 1/2 evidence derives from v0.5 (`--cc-filter off`) and is
  untouched by this change.

## Honest results (test split, off vs auto)

Per-site settings identical between arms (ceteris paribus): `--tune-slope`
everywhere except IndianTunnel_cave_1x (fixed `--slope-mask-degrees 10`,
per the v0.4 caveat); tuned slopes: NorthSurface/Collapse3/Fieg_A/Sheepridge
45°, cave_10x 10°, Kingsbowl 20°. Full precision in the evaluation JSON.

| Site | Rung | F1 off | F1 auto | ΔF1 | P off | P auto | R off | R auto |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IndianTunnel_NorthSurface (cliff) | 1.0 m | **0.3618** | 0.3590 | **−0.0028** | 0.4337 | 0.4430 | 0.3103 | 0.3017 |
| IndianTunnel_Collapse3 (real tube) | 0.5 m | **0.1875** | 0.1875 | 0.0000 | 0.1038 | 0.1038 | 0.9693 | 0.9693 |
| Fieg_A | 0.5 m | **0.1365** | 0.1365 | 0.0000 | 0.0783 | 0.0783 | 0.5306 | 0.5306 |
| Sheepridge | 5.0 m | **0.0909** | 0.0909 | 0.0000 | 0.1111 | 0.1111 | 0.0769 | 0.0769 |
| IndianTunnel_cave_10x | 5.0 m | **0.0847** | 0.0847 | 0.0000 | 0.0442 | 0.0442 | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| Kingsbowl | 5.0 m | **0.0433** | 0.0433 | 0.0000 | 0.0222 | 0.0222 | 0.9333 | 0.9333 |
| IndianTunnel_cave_1x (full res) | 5.0 m | **0.0678** | 0.0678 | 0.0000 | 0.0351 | 0.0351 | 1.000 | 1.000 |

Mechanism (from the audit fields in the JSON):

- **No-op at 6/7 sites**: `cc_components_dropped_ratio = 0.0` in BOTH arms
  at every site except NorthSurface — the post-threshold mask enters the
  filter as a single surviving component (at the thr=0 tuner-collapse
  sites the whole above-threshold surface is one component by
  construction; pinned by
  `test_cc_filter_modes.py::test_cc_filter_survives_thr0_predict_all`).
- **NorthSurface (1 m rung)** is the only multi-component case: `auto`'s
  stricter table min (20 cells vs legacy 5) raises the drop ratio
  0.741 → 0.926, trading +0.0093 precision for −0.0086 recall — net
  **−0.0028 F1**.

## Why the default is off (decision rule, applied verbatim)

> default ON only if `F1_auto >= F1_off − 0.005` at EVERY site AND
> `F1_auto > F1_off` at >= 3 sites; else OFF.

Outcome: non-regressing **7/7** (the −0.0028 at NorthSurface sits inside
the 0.005 slack), improved **0/7** (needs ≥3) → **default OFF**.

This confirms the v0.2-era verdict — the component filter is
**documentation, not lift** on this benchmark (cf. the v0.4 note §5 table:
"F1 raw" vs "F1 +cc (v0.2)" differ at exactly one rung, where cc HURT,
0.162 → 0.057). The capability is retained for future multi-component
score rasters (e.g. fragmented masks on lunar DTM runs, where a
scale-aware per-rung minimum is the principled tool).

- **No change to score formulation** (v0.1 depth × Frangi), **threshold
  tuning** (v0.1 cal-half sweep), **slope tuning** (v0.4), or the
  **illumination/sensor degradation ladder** (v0.5).
- **No change to default-path behaviour**: default `--cc-filter off`
  reproduces v0.5 masks exactly and all 7 site F1s within 2e-3.
- **No frozen evidence touched**: Paper 1/2 JSONs derive from v0.5.
