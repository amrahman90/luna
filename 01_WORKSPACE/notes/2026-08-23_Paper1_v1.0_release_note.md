### Two algorithmic improvements to `score_raster_gen.py`

1. **True fractional rasterio rebin** for non-integer rebin factors (2.5× for 2 m → 5 m rung, replacing incorrectly-rounded 2×). Prevents aliasing.
2. **Depth output on the requested rung grid** (not the 5000-pixel Frangi sub-sampled grid). For TYCHOPK 2 m rung, depth is full DTM extent (30892 × 12240), not sub-sampled (5000 × 1981).

### Skeptic fall-back annotation rule (NEW)

Per skeptic Cycle 1 second-opinion (2026-08-23):

> A candidate is classified `deep-pit low-vesselness` iff:
> 1. `frangi@score_max < 0.02` (Frangi vesselness at the score-maximum pixel is very low — NOT tubular)
> 2. `depth@score_max ≥ 100 m` (the depression is deep)

Threshold rationale: at <0.05 the rule wrongly captures TYCHOPK02 (frangi@score=0.054, a central-peak-relief FP); at <0.02 only MARIUSCONE (0.011) and GRUITHMARE2 (0.015) qualify. The <0.02 threshold cleanly separates the new deep-pit family from the pre-existing central-peak-relief family.

Applied to 12 of 21 new rows (10 GRUITHMARE2 + 2 MARIUSCONE); 6 GRUITHUIS17 rows correctly NOT annotated (frangi@score=0.0424 > 0.02); 3 TYCHOPK rows classified terrain_extrapolation (per existing TYCHOPK02/03/04/07 precedent).

Implementation: `01_WORKSPACE/code/wp2_sag/transfer/apply_skeptic_annotation.py` (Cycle 1, idempotent) + `apply_skeptic_annotation_tychopk.py` (Cycle 2, idempotent).
