# Outreach — Email Templates

> Three templates for the cold-outreach cycle. Replace `[PLACEHOLDER]` blocks per recipient.
> **Tone**: short, specific, question-led. Not a sales pitch; not a request for supervision. A 20-minute conversation is the goal of cold email #1.

---

## Template 1 — Initial Cold Outreach

**Subject**: Calibrated inference under sparse labels — methodology question, short version of project below

**Length**: ~250 words. Attach the PDF of `papers/project_overview_v1.md`.

```
Dear Prof. [LAST NAME],

I'm a CSE student who has spent the last year building an open research
program in calibrated multi-evidence inference — applied to lunar lava
tubes as a test case. I'm preparing PhD applications for Sept 2027
intake (deadlines Dec/Jan 2026-27) and would value your perspective on
whether the framing fits your group's research.

The short version: 62 research sessions produced a 278-row candidate
registry (frozen FP 3.74 [1.71, 7.10] per 10⁴ km², calibration-context)
and a three-regime roof-deformation forward model that reframes the
"search for roof sag" premise — intact elastic flexure is
sub-detection-floor; only the near-collapse regime peaks above the
4-5 m NAC DTM floor. The methodology contributions (PU learning under
distribution shift, calibrated coverage, scale-bridging fusion across
four orders of magnitude) apply beyond the lunar case.

I read your [SPECIFIC PAPER / TALK] and the [SPECIFIC METHODOLOGY]
felt relevant to a question LUNARVOID raises: [THE QUESTION — pick
one from the faculty-list angle OR craft your own].

A one-page project overview is attached. The open repository is at
github.com/amrahman90/luna — code, paper drafts, Zenodo deposit prep.
I'm not asking for supervision — I'd just value a 20-minute
conversation if the methodological fit is genuine.

Best,
[YOUR NAME]
[email] · [LinkedIn URL]
```

## Quick personalisation checklist

For each faculty, before sending:
- [ ] Replace `[LAST NAME]`, `[SPECIFIC PAPER]`, `[SPECIFIC METHODOLOGY]`, `[THE QUESTION]`
- [ ] Pick a [THE QUESTION] that is genuinely interesting to ask (not a softball; not rhetorical)
- [ ] Verify recipient's CURRENT email (institutional move happens; check their most recent paper's corresponding-author line)
- [ ] Proofread for the recipient's name (one misspelling = email goes unread)
- [ ] Confirm the GitHub + portal links work (don't include broken links)
- [ ] Attach the PDF version of `papers/project_overview_v1.md`
- [ ] Send from a polite email address (not `xX_lunar_void_Xx@...`)
- [ ] Send from your personal domain if you have one (university emails for alumni can look odd)
