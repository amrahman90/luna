# Work Queue 02 — SLDEM2015 Cross-Elevation Validation

**Priority:** 3
**Wall time:** ~4 hours (one-shot job over 30 DTMs from #1)
**Cost:** $0 (SLDEM2015 is public; no egress)
**Pre-req:** work-queue #1 (30 random-mare DTMs processed) OR the
existing 10 NAC DTMs from G1.

## Task

Validate that the kriging-correction preserves the long-wavelength
(>300 m) truth against the SLDEM2015 LOLA-derived elevation model.

`code/wp0_kriging/kriging_correction.py` already supports a
cross-validation mode against LOLA tracks; the new addition is a
**raster-to-raster comparison** at the 30-DTM set after #1.

## Why this matters

The kriging correction is documented to preserve pit depth to ±10%
(release notes; conventions §3). The SLDEM2015 cross-check is the
**independent truth**: SLDEM2015 is LOLA-derived at 60°N/S band (no
NAC-DTM bleed); if our NAC DTMs diverge from SLDEM2015 at long
wavelengths, the kriging correction is hiding a real bias.

## Steps

1. SSH to the Hetzner server.
2. Pull SLDEM2015 (already cached under
   `~/lunarvoid/data/index_layers/SLDEM2015_256_60S60N_512_v3.tif`;
   256 ppd, ~1 GB).
3. For each of the 30 DTMs (or the existing 10):
   - Reproject the NAC DTM from the DTM-local eqc CRS to SLDEM2015's
     global eqc CRS (`+proj=eqc +lat_ts=0 +lon_0=0 +R=1737400`).
   - Compute the per-cell residual `nac_dtm - sldem2015`.
   - FFT the residual; report the power spectrum >300 m (low-freq
     band) and 60-300 m (sag-band).
   - Compare the low-freq power against the pre-correction residual.
4. Aggregate per-DTM numbers into a CSV at
   `01_WORKSPACE/data/outputs/wp0_kriging/sldem2015_validation.csv`.

## Success criteria

- All 30 DTMs (or 10 from G1) have a residual FFT power at >300 m
  λ < 1 m² RMS (kriging is low-frequency-neutral).
- 60-300 m sag-band residual RMS < 2 m (the project convention;
  §3 conventions).
- No per-DTM mean bias > 5 m (kriging has not introduced a tilt).
- Report saved to `notes/<date>_sldem2015_validation.md`.

## Output paths

- Per-DTM CSVs: `01_WORKSPACE/data/outputs/wp0_kriging/sldem2015_<dtm>.csv`
- Aggregated CSV: `01_WORKSPACE/data/outputs/wp0_kriging/sldem2015_validation.csv`
- Note: `01_WORKSPACE/notes/<date>_sldem2015_validation.md`
- Updated smoke test: add 1-2 SAG-band RMS regression checks to
  `code/smoke_test.py`.

## Rollback

If the SLDEM2015 cross-check fails (e.g., a DTM has >2 m low-freq
bias), the orchestrator logs it as an anomaly. The kriging
correction does NOT auto-adjust; that would require a re-tuning of
the low-frequency kriging range, which is a v0.6+ task.

## Cost

$0 (SLDEM2015 is cached locally). Only the SSH session wall time
costs the rental.

## Verification

The smoke test gains a new check: per-DTM low-freq RMS < 1 m and
sag-band RMS < 2 m, with a per-DTM PASS/FAIL flag. This is the
**first automated cross-elevation validator** in the project.
